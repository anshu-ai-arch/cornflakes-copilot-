# RB-003: Shot Max Retries Exceeded (SHOT_FAILED_3X)
## Condition
An individual shot ticket within a multi-shot video generation job fails 3 consecutive times due to localized rendering glitches.
## Diagnostic Criteria
- Tool `list_failed_shots` returns one or more shots with `status = FAILED` and `attempts >= 3`.
- Overall job remains blocked waiting on this shot.
## Prescribed Actions
1. Propose `retry_shot` alone without cancelling or requeuing the entire job.
2. Auto-approvable if cost is below $0.50 cap.
