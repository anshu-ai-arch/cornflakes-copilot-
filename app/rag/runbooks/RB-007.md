# RB-007: Provider Recovery After Outage
## Condition
A previously disabled or out-of-funds provider has been topped up and verified healthy.
## Diagnostic Criteria
- Tool `provider_status` returns status HEALTHY and positive credit balance.
## Prescribed Actions
1. Propose `enable_provider` to re-allow dispatch.
2. If an old incident proposed disabling this provider, verify freshness before acting.
