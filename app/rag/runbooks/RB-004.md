# RB-004: Double Charge or Billing Discrepancy
## Condition
Customer was debited more credits than the original generation quote, or billed for stages that were never completed.
## Diagnostic Criteria
- Tool `ledger_for_job` shows total charges exceed quoted amount.
- `net_charged_cents` > `quoted_cost_cents`.
## Prescribed Actions
1. Propose `refund_credits` for exact discrepancy (`net_charged - quoted_cost`).
2. Must never exceed actual charges.
3. Requires named human approval.
