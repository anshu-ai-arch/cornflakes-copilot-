# RB-008: Queue Congestion and Worker Pool Depletion
## Condition
Queue depth exceeds normal operational capacity and wait times spike.
## Diagnostic Criteria
- Tool `queue_depth` reports depth > 50 or average wait > 60 seconds.
## Prescribed Actions
1. Pause low-priority bulk generation queues.
2. Prioritize individual shot retries over full-length movie generations.
