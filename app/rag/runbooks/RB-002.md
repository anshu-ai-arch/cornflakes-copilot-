# RB-002: Worker Lease Expiry and Crash (WORKER_DIED)
## Condition
An asynchronous worker process terminates unexpectedly, loses network connectivity, or misses consecutive heartbeats while executing a video stage.
## Diagnostic Criteria
- Tool `get_job` shows worker lease expired or status unchanged past deadline.
- Tool `recent_logs` records missed heartbeats for over 90 seconds.
## Prescribed Actions
1. Propose `requeue_job` with attempt counter reset.
2. Safe to auto-approve under the standard retry cap.
