# RB-001: Provider Out of Funds (HTTP 402)
## Condition
A third-party video or image generation provider (e.g. Runway, Pika, Luma) returns HTTP 402 Payment Required or error message indicating account credit depletion.
## Diagnostic Criteria
- Tool `provider_status` returns `status = OUT_OF_FUNDS` or balance <= 0.
- Tool `recent_logs` shows 402 Payment Required error trace.
## Prescribed Actions
1. Propose `disable_provider` immediately to prevent new jobs from routing to this provider.
2. Propose `refund_credits` for the customer job that failed, equal to net charged credits.
3. Notify finance and platform ops to top up the account.
